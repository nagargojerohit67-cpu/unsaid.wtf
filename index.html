🌧️ MONSOON GHOSTING CRISIS

INDIA REFRESHES WEATHER APP EVERY 7 MINUTES.
MONSOON SEEN TYPING...

The monsoon entered India like every toxic situationship ever. It arrived, gave everyone hope, then disappeared without explanation. Farmers are waiting. Cities are waiting. Even those Instagram influencers who bought aesthetic umbrellas are waiting.

India's economy still depends heavily on rain in 2026. We have AI, satellites, digital payments, and people ordering onions at 3 AM. Yet half the country still looks at clouds and says, "Bhai, hopefully."

Meteorologists issued a forecast. The forecast was ignored in favour of the group chat uncle who said his knee is hurting, which historically means rain in 48 hours.

Weather apps have been refreshed so many times this week that phone batteries are filing formal complaints. One man in Nagpur checked the humidity percentage at 2:14 AM "just to see."

The IMD has issued updates. The clouds have issued silence. The farmers have issued prayers. The influencers have issued aesthetic rain content using studio fog machines.

Breaking: Indians now trust weather memes more than weather forecasts. Sources confirm the meme that said "Monsoon coming bro, trust" has more engagement than the official satellite report.
